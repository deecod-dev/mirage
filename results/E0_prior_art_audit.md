## 2. In-Depth Analysis of Prior Art

### A. SelfCheckGPT (Manakul et al., EMNLP 2023)
- **Mechanism:** Samples $N$ responses from black-box LLM at temperature $T > 0$; measures consistency using BERTScore, QA overlap, or NLI (DeBERTa).
- **Fault Type:** **100% Natural Hallucinations.** The model generates biographies of individuals from WikiBio; when uncertain, it invents fictional facts naturally.
- **Key Finding for MIRAGE:** SelfCheckGPT operates **only on ungrounded generation** (no retrieval context, no tools). It does not test intermediate pipeline failures (e.g. retriever missing a document, tool parameter failure, query corruption).

### B. RAGTruth (Niu et al., ACL 2024)
- **Mechanism:** Evaluates hallucination detectors (token/span classifiers) against real outputs generated by Llama-2, Mistral, GPT-3.5, and GPT-4 on RAG tasks.
- **Fault Type:** **100% Natural Hallucinations.** Responses were manually annotated by humans at the span level for grounding errors.
- **Key Finding for MIRAGE:** RAGTruth proves that *in single-turn text generation*, natural hallucinations occur and are subtle. However, RAGTruth **does not cover agentic multi-step trajectories** (intermediate subgoal planning, tool invocation failures, tool schema mismatches).

### C. HHEM (Vectara, 2023–2024)
- **Mechanism:** Fine-tuned cross-encoder (T5 / DeBERTa) scoring premise-hypothesis consistency in $[0, 1]$.
- **Fault Type:** **Synthetic NLI Contradictions & Sentence Swaps.** Negative training instances are created by swapping named entities or pairing sentences with mismatched premises (analogous to our T0/T1 tier).
- **Key Finding for MIRAGE:** HHEM detectors are trained on crude entity swaps; their sensitivity drops drastically on subtle, contextual semantic drifts.

### D. ARES (Saad-Falcon et al., NAACL 2024)
- **Mechanism:** Generates synthetic training datasets of Context-Query-Answer triples using few-shot LLM prompts to train DeBERTa-v3 judges for Context Relevance, Faithfulness, and Answer Relevance.
- **Fault Type:** **Synthetic Few-Shot Prompt Injection.** Negative context and unfaithful answers are generated via explicit instructions (e.g. *"Generate a contradictory answer"*).
- **Key Finding for MIRAGE:** ARES is the prime example of the phenomenon MIRAGE diagnoses: detectors trained on synthetic LLM-injected unfaithfulness learn the prompt artifacts of the generator, risking poor generalization to subtle natural failures.

### E. RAGAS (Es et al., EACL 2024)
- **Mechanism:** Automated test set generation via evolutionary prompting (reasoning, conditioning, multi-context) and LLM-as-a-judge scoring.
- **Fault Type:** **Synthetic Evolutionary Prompting.** Faults are created by prompting an LLM to omit required context or mutate statements.
- **Key Finding for MIRAGE:** RAGAS uses synthetic fault injection as ground truth for evaluating RAG pipelines, but never reports or calibrates against injection realism tiers.

## 3. Implications for the MIRAGE Paper (Novelty & Framing Shield)

### ⚠️ The Risk We Just Avoided
If the paper claimed:  
> *"All previous RAG and hallucination detection papers evaluate only on synthetic injected faults."*  
**Reviewer Reaction:** REJECT. Reviewers would cite SelfCheckGPT (EMNLP 2023) and RAGTruth (ACL 2024) to show that natural hallucination datasets exist.

### 🛡️ The Bulletproof Framing to Use
Instead, the paper claims:
1. **Agentic Multi-Step Pipelines Rely on Synthetic Faults:** While single-turn generation datasets (RAGTruth, WikiBio) collect natural hallucinations at high human-annotation cost, **multi-step agentic RAG systems** (involving tool routing, tool parameter execution, iterative retrieval across subgoals) rely almost exclusively on synthetic fault injection (ARES, RAGAS, synthetic diagnostic suites) because natural agent execution traces are prohibitively expensive to curate.
2. **The Injector Realism Gap:** In automated fault detection, detector scores are heavily determined by the *manufacturing method* of the injector (T0 naive string replacement vs T1 published-style prompt swaps vs T2 hardened stealthy faults).
3. **The Fingerprint Phenomenon:** Detectors learn injector-specific implementation artifacts (AUROC > 0.90 on held-out injector attribution) rather than invariant fault representations.
4. **Transparent Diagnostic Contribution:** We provide the first systematic taxonomy of injection realism tiers (T0/T1/T2) and show that standard detectors experience a 20–44 AUROC drop when evaluated on hardened faults, establishing that injection realism must be pre-registered and reported in future RAG benchmark papers.
